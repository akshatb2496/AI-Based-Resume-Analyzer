# AI-Based Resume Analyzer

## 1. Problem Statement

Making a good resume is challenging, for many students and job seekers. In many cases, despite having significant information in your resume it might miss out on including the skills, qualification, and keywords required for a particular job role.

The AI-Based Resume Analyzer aims at analyzing the resume and providing insights on the content included in the resume. The key features of the analyzer include extracting important information such as skills, education, experience, and keywords from a resume and comparing it with the requirements of a selected job role to identify strengths and weaknesses of the resume.

---

## 2. Scope of the Project

The AI-Based Resume Analyzer project encompasses the following:

Upload and process a resume.

Extract text and useful information from the resume.

Extract important skills, education, experience, and keywords.

Analyze the resume according to the selected job role.

Identify relevant and missing skills and keywords.

Provide relevant suggestions for improving the resume.

Generate an easy-to-read report with the analysis.

Provide a simple and intuitive interface for students and job seekers.

The scope of the project includes analyzing a resume and providing feedback. It does not guarantee selection in a specific organization or job role. Similarly, it is not responsible for any final recruitment decisions made by an organization.

---

## 3. Target Users

Following are the potential target users for the system.

Students: To build and analyze their resumes for internships and placements.

Fresh Graduates: To prepare their resumes for the first-time job applications.

Job Seekers: For resume suggestions for specific job roles.

Career Beginners: For guidance on relevant skills and information to add to a resume.

Placement Candidates: For preparing and analyzing a resume for job placements.

---

## 4. High-Level Features

The AI-Based Resume Analyzer system offers the following key features.

1. Resume Upload

Enabling the user to upload a resume document for analysis.

2. Resume Text Extraction

Extracting the text contained within the resume.

3. Information Extraction

Extracting relevant information such as skills, education, experience, and projects.

4. Keyword Analysis

Extracting relevant keywords based on a target job role.

5. Skill-based Analysis

Comparing and contrasting the skills mentioned in the resume with the skills required for the selected job role.

6. Missing Skill Analysis

Identifying the relevant skills missing from a resume.

7. Resume Suggestions

Providing suggestions for improvement of the content and information included in the resume.

8. Resume Analysis Report

Generating an easy-to-read resume analysis report based on the job role.

9. Job Role Selection

Providing an option to select or type the target job role for the analysis.

10. Easy-to-Use Interface

Enabling the user to easily upload a resume document and the analysis results.
